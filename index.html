HOW I WORK

Built like it has to last

Most of what makes a project go well happens before anyone opens an editor. This is the order I work in, and what you get at each step.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    What the thing is for, who it is for, and what counts as done. Scope goes in writing before anything is estimated.

    You get — a written scope and a fixed price

  2. 02

    Architecture

    Data model, states and edge cases mapped first. The dull decisions made early, so the interesting ones stay cheap.

    You get — a schema, a state map, a stack you own

  3. 03

    Build

    Shipped in reviewable slices, each one deployable on its own. You see it working weekly, not at the end.

    You get — a staging URL from week one

  4. 04

    Handover

    Documented, tested and yours. No black boxes, and nothing that needs me to keep it running.

    You get — the repo, a walkthrough, and support after

STEP BY STEP

From first message to a repo that’s yours

Four moves. You see it running in week one, and nothing at the end needs me around to keep it alive.

  1. 01

    The brief lands

    Send whatever you have — a doc, a Figma, or three paragraphs. You get a reply the same day with the questions I’d need answered to quote it properly.

  2. 02

    We pin the scope down

    One call, then it goes in writing. Fixed price, named deliverables, and the awkward edge cases agreed before they are expensive rather than after.

  3. 03

    You watch it ship

    Reviewable slices, deployed as they land. A staging URL from week one, so you are never waiting until the end to find out what you bought.

  4. 04

    You own the whole thing

    The repo, the documentation and a recorded walkthrough. No black boxes, and no part of it that quietly depends on me still being here.
