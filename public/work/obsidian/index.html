  • Cut

    Patterns are cut a full size over, then washed back until the cloth settles into its own shape.

    01

  • Weight

    Nothing under 380gsm. A garment should hold its own line without being asked to.

    02

  • Finish

    Raw hems, bar-tacked at every stress point, and not one visible logo anywhere on the piece.
