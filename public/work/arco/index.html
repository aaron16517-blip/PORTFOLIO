Storia

Our aim was simple: bring the experience of real Italian cooking to this corner of the city. Every plate leaves the pass the way it would leave a kitchen at home — unhurried, generous, and made for the table it is going to.

What you taste is the meeting point of a traditional kitchen and years of modern hospitality. The recipes did not change. The room around them did.

Amore e appetito

We know: love goes through the stomach.

We do not think of what we serve as simply food. Because every dish is built with patience and real attention, it carries a way of living with it — the feeling of sitting with a glass of wine and letting the evening take its time.

That is the whole idea. Arco is the place for it.
